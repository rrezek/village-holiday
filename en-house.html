A luxury villa for seven, the last house in the village

Three bedrooms, three bathrooms, a living room with a fireplace and a covered terrace with a stone barbecue. The whole villa and garden are yours alone.

Ground floor: living room, kitchen, dining area and bathroom

An open-plan space with a large table, comfortable sofas and a stone fireplace. The kitchen is fully equipped, from the espresso machine to the dishwasher.

The living room opens straight onto the covered terrace. The ground floor also has a bathroom with a shower.

Upstairs: three bedrooms and a bathroom with a bathtub

Upstairs you will find three bedrooms under wooden beams and a bathroom with a bathtub.

  • Bedroom 1: double bed (140 × 200 cm) and a single bed
  • Bedroom 2: two single beds
  • Bedroom 3: two single beds

Outside: terrace, barbecue and a large garden

A covered terrace with a long table and a stone barbecue with a grill, a peka (traditional bell-shaped baking dish) and a cauldron. Firewood for the barbecue and fireplace is included, and more can be bought if needed.

The garden has a children's playhouse and a fenced playground.

The lawn is big enough for football and badminton.

When it snows, the garden becomes a snowball battlefield while the terrace stays dry for the barbecue.

Free parking is on the property, and the path to the house is lit.
