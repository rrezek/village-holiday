Between Velebit and five national parks

Lovinac is a peaceful village in the Lika region at the foot of Velebit. Mountains, caves, rivers and the sea are all within easy reach, and in the evening you return to the quiet.

National parks

All five parks are easy day trips.

  • Plitvice Lakes

    45 min

    Sixteen lakes linked by waterfalls, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. In winter, under snow and ice, they look completely different.

    Tickets and information

  • Paklenica

    40 min

    The canyons of Velika and Mala Paklenica on the southern slopes of Velebit. A renowned climbing area and the start of many hiking trails.

    Park website

  • Northern Velebit

    1 h 10 min

    Zavižan, the Velebit Botanical Garden and the Premužić Trail. A full day in the mountains.

    Park website

  • Krka

    1 h 5 min

    Waterfalls on the Krka river, including Skradinski buk. A good outing even on hot days.

    Park website

  • Kornati

    1 h 20 min plus boat

    An archipelago of more than eighty islands. Boat trips depart from Murter and nearby towns.

    Park website

Nearby excursions

For days when you don't want to go far: caves, Tesla, adventure and a river.

In winter, the nearest fun is the Cvituša sledding hill, 3 minutes from the villa.

  • Cvituša

    3 min

    A picnic area on the edge of the village with a sledding hill. Perfect for children in winter, with a restaurant next door.

    Cvituša on Facebook

  • Cerovac Caves

    25 min

    One of the largest cave systems in Croatia, with guided tours.

    Tours and prices

  • Nikola Tesla Memorial Centre

    35 min

    Nikola Tesla's birthplace and museum in Smiljan, near Gospić.

    Centre website

  • Rizvan City

    40 min

    An adventure park on the edge of Velebit with horse riding and adrenaline activities for the whole family.

    Park website

  • Zrmanja river

    50 min

    Kayaking and rafting through the Zrmanja canyon, departing from Kaštel Žegarski.

    Route on the map

Sea and towns

    30 min

  • Queen's Beach, Nin
